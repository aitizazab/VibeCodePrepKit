Notes on how broadcast news is usually written (to use or ignore):
- **The lead is one complete sentence of what happened**, in the present perfect ("Two people have died..."),
  then the source ("police say..."), then the details, then what's still unknown.
- **Short sentences, said once.** Numbers are spoken as words. Place names are repeated, not replaced with "it".
- **A local anchor often opens with a handoff** ("Good morning. We begin with...") and **closes with a pointer
  forward** ("We'll bring you more as we learn it."). A reader stops sounding like a list once those bookends
  are there.
- Elias stays unnamed in the report (the family hasn't been told yet). That's realistic, and it's the
  dramatic irony: the player knows exactly who it is.
