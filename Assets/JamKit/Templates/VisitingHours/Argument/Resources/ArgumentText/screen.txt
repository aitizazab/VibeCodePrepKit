## The transcript: every step spelled out (a judge reads these)
logic.sound: You said "{premise}". {Evidence} shows: {reason} So "{premise}" doesn't hold.
  ? a premise broken by a sound reason
logic.beside: "{reason}" may be true, but it doesn't answer "{premise}".
  ? a reason that's true but beside the point
logic.nonSequitur: "{reason}" doesn't follow from {evidence}.
  ? a reason that doesn't follow
logic.antagonise: That dodged "{premise}" and attacked {opponent} instead. {opponent} is angrier, and her pressure will be harder.
logic.retort: That dodged "{premise}" and attacked {opponent}. {opponent} is angrier, and her pressure will be harder.
logic.retortQuestion: That dodged the question "{question}" and attacked {opponent}. {opponent} is angrier, and her pressure will be harder.
logic.unbreakable: Nothing Nadia has can answer "{premise}" tonight.
logic.true: "{premise}" is true. Concede it instead: true premises can't be broken.
logic.noTouch: {Evidence} has nothing to do with "{premise}".
logic.concede: Nadia conceded "{premise}": it's true. Her heart slows.
logic.concedeFalse: Nadia agreed with "{premise}", which isn't true. That line is closed now.
logic.press: Nadia forced "{premise}" down without a reason. {opponent} is hurt.
logic.pressClaim: Nadia forced the claim itself. It's over.
