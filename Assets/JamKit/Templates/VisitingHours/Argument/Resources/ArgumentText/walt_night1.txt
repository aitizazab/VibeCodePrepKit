## The coach on the earpiece
coachName: Mags
  ? who's coaching
coach.ask: Ask him why. People tell you more than they mean to when they explain themselves.
  ? the battle has started: ask why
coach.challenge: You've got something that says otherwise. Challenge it: show it, then say why it matters. Pick the reason that actually follows.
  ? a premise she can break is on the table
coach.concede: That part's true. Say so. Agreeing with the truth steadies you, and it costs you nothing.
  ? she has heard a premise that's true
coach.pressure: Your heart's up. Keep your light out of the way and breathe. It passes.
  ? the first pressure phase
coach.press: If you can't break something, you can force it. It works. It also costs, him and you. Use it sparingly.
  ? the first misstep, or only what she can't break is left
coach.rest: You've heard all of it. You can let it rest: tell him you hear him. Sometimes that's enough.
  ? everything's been heard
coach.broken: That's it. When one of their reasons falls, the rest has to stand without it.
  ? the first premise she breaks
