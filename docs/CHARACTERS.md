# Visiting Hours: characters (design reference; the creator's premise of 2026-10-10)

**The dream versions shouldn't feel overwhelmingly wrong** (the creator, 2026-10-10). Being billboards in the
dream while the waking world is full 3D is already eerie enough. Keep them cute, with any unease subtle.

Character sheets and models are drawn from this. Story facts come from the creator; appearance notes are
proposals for the art. Art style **E**: the **waking world in full 3D models** (grounded, muted, stylised but
adult), the **dream as billboards** (cutesy pastel doodle, with dread underneath).

## The world
A near-future city sliding into dystopia: Cyberpunk without the cybernetics. Corporations run the streets and
still pretend to be decent. **LeBaget** is a corporation (it may become a big part of the story: undecided) and
**Larpaca** is a gang; the crash on Route 9 is reported as gang violence. **Saint Brigid's** is a community hospital that shouldn't exist
in this city.

## Nadia (the protagonist)
- **Role:** a nurse at Saint Brigid's; Elias's younger sister.
- **Facts:**
  - She usually picks Elias up from work.
  - The night of the crash she played his voicemail, heard the start, and fell asleep as it played to its end.
  - Her guilt is survivor's guilt (the creator): she believes herself at least partly responsible for losing Lena
    and Theo. What Lena did or was that makes this make sense is undecided.
  - The day before, he'd implied she only got her job because she was his sister. It hurt because it's half
    true: she was qualified, but the other candidate was just as good.
- **Want:** her brother back. **Need:** to forgive herself, or to let go.
- **Look, waking world:** 29; navy St. Brigid's scrubs under a worn city jacket; hair tied back badly; her
  hospital ID badge on a lanyard (it's also the title screen's Settings); tired eyes, guarded posture.
- **Look, dream:** the same person drawn soft and small, eyes a little too round.

## Elias Lasname (surname a placeholder)
- **Role:** an ER doctor, and the man who **founded Saint Brigid's** for the local community. He's in a coma.
- **Facts:**
  - He comes from money. Where it came from stays unsaid: players will assume he was born into it, which leaves
    room for a later answer.
  - A good man with an abrasive personality.
  - The night of the crash he stayed late at work because he was putting off facing Nadia. His car was **caught
    in the crossfire** on Route 9 (reported as gang violence). The cause was external, which leaves room for more
    later.
- **Look, waking world:** 34; sharp, well-kept, expensive-but-practical clothes worn without care (rolled
  sleeves, a good coat with a stain on it). In hospital: bandaged, monitors.
- **Look, dream:** relaxed, smiling, softer than he ever was awake. The dream version is who he wishes he were.

## Lena
- **Role:** Elias's wife. **Missing, not dead** (the creator, 2026-10-12): her body was never found, and where she is
  stays an open thread.
- **Her passion: stargazing** (the creator). Her small observatory is the attic of the house (approved, with a raised
  roof section over it). No ceramics, clay or apron.
- **In the dream:** a memory Elias rebuilds every night, who knows what she is and defends his dream.
- **Look:** early 30s; warm, quick to laugh. In the dream, slightly too perfect (the chosen design is round 4
  below).
- **The creator's note (2026-10-10):** in the first sheets, Lena with her bun looked too much like Nadia. She gets
  **blonde hair or a clearly different hairstyle** (not a bun), so the two never read as the same woman.
- **The rules for Lena's look (the creator, 2026-10-10, round 2).** Lena and Nadia aren't related, so nothing about
  them may echo each other:
  - **A clearly different face from Nadia's.** Nadia is narrow and angular with hollow cheeks. Lena is soft and
    round: full cheeks, a rounded chin, a short button nose, soft rounded brows, large wide-set green-grey eyes, a wide
    mouth, freckles. Never make Lena's face with Nadia's sheet attached.
  - **Her own clothes and silhouette.** Nothing that echoes Nadia's jacket and scrubs or Elias's coat and shirt (a
    different colour family and silhouette).
  - **Sleeves down, always.** Pushed-up sleeves are the siblings' shared quirk (Nadia and Elias); Lena never has it.
  - **The pencil rests on top of her ear**, tucked behind it, with her hair tucked behind that ear so it reads as
    deliberate (not lost in her hair).
  - **Keep:** honey-blonde, long loose waves, freckles. In the dream she stays cute and not overwhelmingly wrong: being
    a billboard in the dream instead of 3D is eerie enough.
- **Round 3 (the creator's notes on round 2, 2026-10-10):**
  - **Fuller, rounder and rested is right**, but round 2 looked like she works hard for a living. **She doesn't need to
    work** (Elias comes from money): what she makes is a hobby, unless it's a real passion. **No workwear, no apron, no
    clay, no tired posture.** Dressed well but not flashy: quality, considered, a little private. Round 3's outfit: a
    cream fine-knit high-neck sweater, a wine-red pleated midi skirt, slim dark boots.
  - **Mysterious:** composed, guarded, a little unreadable, a private life, so that her going **missing** stays a real
    open question and not an allegory for death. Through expression, posture and styling (a half-smile that doesn't
    explain itself, a closed notebook, a watch worn face-in, an old brass key on a chain), never anything sinister.
  - **The nose must belong to the face:** shaded into it, no separate outline (round 2's looked pasted on).
  - **The pencil shows a curious, ordered mind:** neat, sharpened, always in the same place (a slim dark-green pencil
    on the right ear), the habit of someone who notes things down and keeps things in order.
  - **In the dream, her ear is small and sits close to the head** under the hair, never sticking out; the pencil stays
    visible.
  - Sheets: round 2 in `docs/art/vh-lena3-picks.json`, round 3 in `docs/art/vh-lena4-picks.json`.
- **Round 4: CHOSEN (the creator, 2026-10-10): "4-a is what we'll work with."** Sheets in `docs/art/vh-lena5-picks.json`:
  waking turnaround `lena5-turn-take1`, dream billboard `lena5-bb-take2`, dream expressions `lena5-expr-take2`.
  - **The fixes that made 4-a:** the key necklace is gone (it could read as a cross; nothing on her chest or neck, only
    the watch worn face-in); the notebook is held loosely in one hand, never clasped to her chest (that read religious);
    the skirt became slim deep wine-red wool trousers with dark-brown lace-up ankle boots (she goes out at night, so the
    clothes are practical).
  - **Why not the other designs:** the fresh design and the reconciled versions lost what the creator liked about 4-a:
    **the sketchbook look, the unique posing and the strength of the defining colour.** Keep those in every future
    sheet of her.
  - **Height:** her dream billboard reads about 6'2" against the rest of the cast's 5'8"–5'11" (Theo smaller). The
    creator: 6'2" is fine.

## Theo
- **Role:** Elias and Lena's son, ten, killed in the crash. In the dream, he's the one who asks the question that
  matters: "Is Dad going to be sad?" Whether Theo stays in the story is undecided (the creator is considering cutting
  him; they liked his Night 2 hide-and-seek and his torch on Night 4).
- **Look (a proposal):** ten; a gap-toothed grin; a hoodie too big for him. In the dream, the most cutesy figure
  of all.

## Mags (Marguerite Ilunga)
- **Role:** runs the **secret, off-the-books** Dream Bridge after visiting hours; the mentor. Frank about how the
  rig works, evasive about the nine dives that went wrong.
- **Look, waking world:** 50s; close-cropped grey hair; reading glasses on a cord; a cardigan over scrubs; a mug
  of cold coffee that never leaves her hand.

## Walt Abernathy
- **Role:** the dream's first guardian. The old neighbour who taught Elias to fix bikes, dead since 2015.
- **Look, dream only:** a tall, thin old man; flat cap; a grease-stained apron; a white moustache; wiping his
  hands on a red rag.

## A note on names
Both names on Route 9 are fictional: **LeBaget** (the corporation) and **Larpaca** (the gang). No real company may be
shown in a negative light. The spelling is LeBaget (confirmed by the creator, 2026-10-12).
