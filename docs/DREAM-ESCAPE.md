# The dream escape: Visiting Hours' first chase

Nadia runs from the dream's immune system (**the static**: a wall of pastel TV snow and its "wasps") down
the dream version of Walt's street, on the Titanfall-style movement module (`docs/MOVEMENT.md`). Art style
E: the dream is **real 3D**, a too-perfect golden hour in a cutesy pastel doodle look with dread
underneath; the neighbours and the dog are **flat cut-outs** (billboards exist only in the dream) that turn
to watch you pass. About 45 s for the autopilot, 60-90 s for a player on a first run.

It plays **standalone** until the story wires it in: **JamKit > Use Template > Dream Escape**, then Play
(switch back with **Use Template > Visiting Hours**). Batch:
`-executeMethod JamKit.DreamEscape.EditorTools.DreamEscapeTemplate.UseFromCommandLine`.

## The chase, beat by beat

The street runs along +Z, so distance along the route is s = z. The lane (where the autopilot runs, and
where the warm pools lie) moves across the street. Times are the autopilot's (sprint 9.5 m/s); a first-time
player is slower and slips once or twice.

| s (m) | Beat | What you do | Sizes | Autopilot at |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 18, 30, 42 | **The fences** | Hop three picket fences across the street (a missed hop is a mantle). Past the first, collapse 1: the fences fold flat in a wave that overtakes you along the right-hand pavement. | 0.85 m tall; jump about 3 m before each | 1.7-4.8 s |

| 75-98 | Street | Drift to the left kerb. The dog sits at the edge of the void, watching. | street 1 m lower | |
| 98-123 | **Car roofs** | The road has peeled up into the void; three toy cars are the way across: hop on, roof to roof, off. | roofs 1.05 m; 4.4 m long; 3 m apart; 3.8 m to the street | 10.0-13.0 s |
| 123-160 | **The barbecue** (checkpoint) | A breather: cross the street past the neighbour at his grill (and his smoke). | lane -4.3 to +7.6 over 30 m | 13-16.8 s |

Floor heights: the street is 0 at the start, -1 after the first wall, -2 after the garden wall; roofs
+2.2, then +1.2, +0.2, the low roof -2.8 and the lit porch -3.8. **Every wall jump lands down onto the next
surface**: a landing at the take-off height comes up short after a 14 m wall (the wallrun sinks), and the
mantle that rescues it costs the speed.

### The chase
- **The pursuer** (core `Pursuer`): rubber-banded along the route, target gap 12 m, k 0.6, 6 to 11.5 m/s;
  it catches only after 0.75 s under 2 m, so it never surprises a player who keeps moving.
- **Seeing it:** a curved wall of static 17 m tall across the street, its sides reaching forward, with 40
  wasps boiling ahead of it; and **scouts** that fly at 46-60 degrees off your gaze, 4-7 m out (always 2,
  up to 6 as it closes in), so it is in your peripheral vision even when you look ahead.
- **Hearing it:** the static's hiss from the wall (spatial, louder and higher as it closes), a buzz near you
  (from a murmur to loud), and the **chase music** (`DreamChaseMusic`, PLACEHOLDER, D minor, 104 bpm): a pad
  throughout (its low-pass opens as it nears), a pulse with a soft kick that grows from 12 % to full, and a
  music box that gets louder and slides out of tune when the static is right behind you.
- **Failing forgives:** caught, or fallen into the void (below y -14): a 0.15 s white-out and you're back at
  the last checkpoint **running at sprint**, the static 12 m behind, collapses after the checkpoint put back.
  After 2 fails at one checkpoint the static quietly gives 4 m more room and 0.8 m/s less speed there.
- **Checkpoints:** Walt's driveway (0), The barbecue (128), Rooftops (246), Final run (374): 11-13 s apart.
- **Collapses** fire by position (`LabCollapse.triggerS`) and travel as a wave (`DreamWave`: start
  offset from the trigger, speed): the fences at s 25 (wave from s 12 at 17 m/s, fold flat; only the
  fence behind you is solid), the house fronts at s 303 (wave from s 293 at 16 m/s, peel). Solid pieces
  only ever collapse behind you; the rest are beside the route.

### Readability
- **The route is lit warmer:** soft gold pools of light on the ground along the lane every 4.5 m (not on
  the ground under a wall run: there the route is the wall).
- **One "go" colour** (pastel gold), only for traversal: a stripe along every runnable wall (and only those
  run: `onlyMarkedWalls`), a lip on every ledge you climb or land on, the glow under the garage door.
- **The exit is the brightest thing**: the sun sits low ahead, and the light at the end is a pillar.
- Space: the street is 60 m wide, the roofs 12 m; nothing decorative stands within 1.2 m of the lane
  (tested).

## The look
- `JamKit/Dream/Pastel`: flat pastel colour lit in three soft steps by a low golden-hour sun with a cool
  lilac shade, a crayon grain, and a wobbly doodle outline along every box edge (from the object-space
  position of Unity's cube or quad, so scaled boxes keep 5 cm lines). Below the street everything sinks
  into the lilac void. Unlit: no lights or shadows needed.
- `Sky`: lilac overhead, a peach horizon, a big soft sun ahead, crayon clouds; below the horizon the void
  with slow doodle stars. Fog: linear, peach, 45-230 m.
- Houses: 12 pastels with coral, plum, teal or apricot gable roofs, mostly dark windows with the odd warm
  one, white doors. Runnable walls are **flats**: house fronts with nothing behind them.

## Tuning it

- **Movement numbers** live in `Dream/Data/DreamEscapeTuning.asset` (a `MovementTuning`; edit it in Play
  mode). The EditMode test `TheRouteFitsTheReachOfTheTuningTheSceneUses` fails if a change makes a gap,
  wall, hop or climb out of reach (`DreamEscapeCourse.CheckReach`).
- **The chase** in `DreamEscapeCourse.Make()`: `c.pursuer` (gap, k, speeds), `wakeAfter`, `easeAfterFails`,
  `easeGap`.
- **Sizes and places** in `Make()`; run `dotnet test core-tests/DreamEscape/DreamEscape.Tests.csproj` after
  every change: the autopilot runs it at 20, 30, 60, 144 and 300 fps and with jittery frames, from every
  checkpoint, and prints a trace (time, z, x, y, state, speed) and each section's average speed.
- **In the game:** F3 (View) shows the speed HUD (speed, state, the speed graph, section, run time, the
  chase gap). Every respawn logs `JAMKIT_DREAM respawn n: why` with the last 8 state changes.
- **Music and wasps:** `DreamChaseMusic.volume`, `DreamStaticView.hissVolume/buzzVolume/scouts`.

## Building the next escape

1. **Block it out as beats** on paper: a run-up, then one new move at a time (safe first, then over the
   void, then chained), a breather every 20-30 s, a checkpoint every 10-20 s, two collapses at most.
2. **Write it as data**: a new `static Build...()` next to `DreamEscapeCourse.Make()` using the same
   helpers: `Floor`, `RunWall`, `Fence`, `Car`, `House`/`HouseRow`, `Add(name, kind, look, min, max)`,
   `Checkpoint`, `Lane`, `Cue`, `Beat`, `Billboard`, and `c.collapses` + `waves` with a piece's `collapse`
   index. Copy the proven offsets:

   | Move | Offsets that work (relative) |
   |---|---|
   | Single gap | floor ends at E; jump cue at E - 0.8; landing at E + 5.5 |
   | Double gap | jump cue E - 0.8, double jump cue E; landing E + 8.5 |
   | Wallrun over a gap | lane 0.9 m off the face from 10 m before; floor ends E; wall E + 3 to E + 17; jump cue E - 0.8; wall jump cue 4.5 m before the wall's end; landing a metre lower from the wall's end + 1 |
   | Wall to wall | walls 5 m apart: left E + 1 to E + 13, right E + 7 to E + 25; cues: jump E - 0.8, wall jumps E + 1.6 and E + 20.5, double jump E + 21; lanes from the lab's finale |
   | Hop (fence) | 0.85 m tall; jump cue 3 m before |
   | Car hop | roof 1.05 m, 4.4 m long, 3 m apart; cues 3.6 m before the first car, then 1.2 m before each roof's end |
   | Slide under | gap 1.15 m; crouch cue from 6 m before to 2 m after |
   | Mantle climb | blocks 6 m deep, each 1.0-1.6 m higher, no cues |
   | Slide ramp | 16 m, 3 m drop; crouch cue from 2 m before to its end |

3. **Prove it in dotnet** before Unity: copy `DreamEscapeCourseTests` (autopilot at any frame rate, every
   checkpoint to the next, reach, decor off the lane, collapses behind). Read the trace: a `Mantle` right
   after a wall jump means the landing is too high or too far.
4. **Build it**: point a builder at it (the `DreamEscapeBuilder` dresses any piece by its `DreamLook`), give
   it a scene name, presenter chapters and a template switch, and add the EditMode/PlayMode tests.
5. **Wire it into the story**: `DreamEscapeGame.Escaped` fires when Nadia reaches the light; with a listener
   the escape stops there (no loop) and the story takes over.

## Known gaps
- **Placeholders:** the cast's cut-outs, the wasp mask, the chase music and Nadia's arms until the style-E art
  and the score land. The static's hiss is the movement module's placeholder `mv_static`.
- Not yet wired into the slice's story flow (the `Escaped` event is the hook).
- The route is straight down the street (the course data's route is a straight line along +Z); turns
  would need the views to follow `PathLine` directions (the core supports polylines).
- No real-time shadows (unlit look by design); the arms are lit by one warm directional light.
- No difficulty setting beyond the quiet easing after repeated fails.
