# Lena, a fresh design (option B, 2026-10-10)

An independent second option, drawn up without looking at any earlier Lena art, prompt or rule.
Prompts: `docs/art/prompts/lena-fresh/`. Takes: `FableAnimationTests/art/incoming/lenafresh-*`.

## Who she reads as
Elias's wife and Theo's mother, about thirty-five. Comfortable, unhurried, well looked-after: she
has never had to look tired. Composed and a little unreadable: she is pleasant to you, and part of
her attention is always somewhere else. Nothing sinister: she looks like a woman with a private
life, not a woman with a secret crime.

## Silhouette
One long, soft vertical: long loose blonde waves past the shoulder blades, a knee-length open
cardigan-coat that falls straight, wide-leg trousers to the ankle. Very upright, level head, still
hands, weight even on both feet. Against Nadia (boxy short jacket, hunched, hair scraped back) she
is long, loose-haired, straight-backed and calm.

## Palette (no olive, no navy)
- dusty heather-plum (the long cardigan-coat): her one colour
- warm oatmeal / cream (the fine high-neck sweater)
- warm stone / taupe (the trousers)
- dark oxblood-brown (the loafers)
- honey-ash blonde hair; one muted ochre accent: the pencil

All desaturated to sit in the waking world's grey light.

## Clothes
- A knee-length dusty heather-plum soft wool cardigan-coat, worn open, no belt, deep patch pockets, **sleeves
  down to the wrists**.
- Under it a warm oatmeal-cream fine-knit mock-neck sweater, **sleeves down** too.
- High-waisted wide-leg stone trousers; flat oxblood leather loafers.
- A thin gold wedding band; a slim watch with a dark face, **worn face-in** on the inside of her
  left wrist (the habit of someone who checks the time in the dark).
- In one coat pocket, the top of a small dark cloth-bound notebook.
- The one off note: a little pale dust on the edges of her loafers' soles, as if she's walked
  somewhere that isn't pavement.

## Face
- Fuller, rounder and rested: soft full cheeks, a rounded jaw and chin, clear healthy skin, a little
  colour in the cheeks, **no dark circles, no hollows**.
- Calm grey-green eyes, slightly heavy-lidded, soft natural brows a shade darker than her hair.
- **The nose belongs to the face:** small and soft with a rounded tip, drawn with the same few light
  lines as her eyes and mouth, its shading continuous with her cheeks; no hard outline around it.
- A closed mouth with the faintest resting half-smile: pleasant, not telling you anything.

## Hair and the pencil
Honey-ash blonde, long loose waves, a soft side parting. On her **right** side the hair is tucked
neatly behind the ear, and a short, well-sharpened ochre pencil rests on top of that ear. The tuck
is deliberate and tidy: curious and ordered. (The first prompt asked for the left ear, but the model drew
the side view facing right and put the pencil on her right ear in every view, so the right ear it is. The dream prompts say right.) In the dream, that ear is tiny and flat against the head.

## Her passion: the night sky
She keeps an **observing log**: she drives out past the city lights on clear nights, alone, and notes
what she sees, in pencil (ink smudges in the cold; observers use pencil).
It gives her a real, quiet passion that fits "curious and ordered", and it makes "somewhere else she
goes" literal, which is exactly what keeps her disappearance open.

In the look it stays subtle: the pencil, the notebook in the pocket, the watch worn face-in, the dust
on her shoes. No telescope, no star prints, no moon jewellery.

## How the mystery shows
- **Expression:** a resting half-smile that doesn't explain itself; in three-quarter views her gaze
  sits just past the viewer.
- **Posture:** stillness. Upright, hands quiet, nothing fidgety.
- **Styling:** the face-in watch, the notebook, the dusty shoe edges: small things that say she has
  a life outside the house.
- **Dream:** she is the one figure who doesn't quite look at you. She holds her notebook closed against
  her chest and glances up and to the side, as if listening to something far away. Pastel and sweet,
  but too still.

## The sheets
1. Waking turnaround: front, side, back, A-pose (style reference: Mags's turnaround, whose sleeves are down; Elias's has rolled sleeves the model could copy). The coat is plum, not oatmeal, so she doesn't echo Mags's long oatmeal cardigan.
2. Dream billboard (references: the chosen turnaround + Theo's billboard for the style).
3. Dream expressions: warm, knowing, defensive (reference: the chosen billboard).

## Round 4 (2026-10-10): the creator's notes applied
- Eyes **green**, gaze **level**: no upward or sideways glance (that read playful). The mystery is
  in a calm, half-lidded, unreadable half-smile.
- **One notebook**: the pockets are flat and empty, and the notebook is held loosely in one hand at
  her hip (never clasped to the chest, which read religious). Turnarounds are empty-handed (A-pose).
- Waking and dream share the same honey-ash hair, the plum coat, cream sweater, stone trousers and
  oxblood loafers.
- Prompts: `lenafresh2-*` (this design, fixed); `lenafresh-recon2-turn`, `-recon3-bb`,
  `-recon3-expr` (the other design's Lena in these clothes; give the model only her sheet and
  describe the clothes in words: given both Lenas as pictures, it copied this design's face).
