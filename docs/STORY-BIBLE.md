## 0. The spine on one page
**What happened:** a man stayed late to avoid his sister, called her at 3 AM, and she heard him ask and went back to
sleep. So he called his wife, who brought their sleeping son, and on the way home a war between two corporations (// corpo and a gang, but it doesn't matter within the scope of this game) went through their car. The people who fired the guns face nothing. The two people who love each other most each believe they did it.

**The question for the player:** what do we owe the people we love, their happiness or their life? And how much of
what we call love is need? (Draft 1's message, unchanged.) The new premise sharpens it: **Nadia is the one person
he trusted to decide for him**, and she can't tell whether she wants him back for his sake or to be forgiven.

**Both sides at their strongest (the no-right-answer test):**

| Wake him | Let him stay |
|---|---|
| He's alive, and grief can be lived through. It's his to go through, not to be spared | He'd wake to his son's grave, a wife missing with no answers, a ruined right hand that ends his medicine, and the belief that he called them to their deaths |
| His body is failing under the dream, and every night costs his heart | The dream is the only place his family still exists, and he built it himself: it's his choice, made the only way he can make it |
| St. Brigid's, his life's work, is being quietly absorbed without him | He named Nadia to decide, and left "what I'd want" blank. Nobody knows his answer |
| His sister needs him, and so do his patients | "Needing him" is exactly the selfishness he'd see through |

No oppressor sits inside the dilemma. The corporations are the cause and the background; they never become the
answer to the question.

**The two guilts (the emotional engine):**
- **Nadia's:** she heard "You still good for picking me up?" and chose sleep, because of what he'd said the day
  before. Because she didn't come, he called Lena.
- **Elias's:** he stayed late to avoid her, then called his wife and son out at 3 AM, and insisted on driving.

Each thinks the night is theirs. Neither has told anyone. The chosen path to Morning is the two of them saying it
out loud and finding out the other one has been carrying the same night.

## 3. Night by night

### Night 1: The Street
**The day (Day 1, six weeks after; the tutorial day).**
- Nadia arrives for her shift. **Rafael** hands over the night ("He had a quiet one."). He's been covering her extra
  hours. The duty loop is taught here: one meds round, read the wristbands, chart the vitals.
- **Room 14:** the chart (`fact_chart`: no reason he shouldn't wake), the effects bag (`fact_effects`: a keyring tagged
  "W.A. workshop", a memorial card "Walter Abernathy, 1941–2015"), the family photo (`fact_photo`), and a bouquet with
  a LeBaget card: "With the deepest respect, C. Vasse, Community Partnerships".
- **The police envelope** at the station (`fact_police_report`): the timeline, "passenger door found open", "L. Lasname:
  missing", and the call log (3:04 to N. Lasname, rang out; 3:07 to L. Lasname, 2 min). **Nobody comments on it.**
- **Her phone** (`fact_voicemail`): "Elias, 0:41, played to 0:09". Selecting it: "Not now." She won't play it, all game,
  until an ending. The player knows what's on it.
- **The optional deduction** (the bomb, for players who look): the call log plus the voicemail's first line gives
  `ded_why_lena_came`: *"He called Lena three minutes after me. Because I didn't come."* Nadia scribbles it out
  (legible if you look closely). Players who don't make it here hear it from Lena on Night 2.
- In the staff room, a poster: "Who decides for you? Name your person. Advance Care Planning Week." Never pointed at.
- **11 PM, the closed sleep-research wing.** Mags, the booth, the rig, the journal. A curtained bed in the dark
  (bed 3): "Don't." The first phone ritual: "Plug it in?" (innocuous; most will say yes).

**The dive.** Larch Street on a Saturday at golden hour: lawns, sprinklers, a barbecue, Biscuit the dog, Mrs. Lindqvist
at her roses, Mr. Okafor at the grill. **It's the street the Lasname kids grew up on**: Walt's bike shop at the bottom,
their father's house on the hill at the top, gate chained, curtains drawn. Elias is at the barbecue, laughing, always
just too far away to reach.
- Movement wakes up ("You're lighter in here." Mags): the creek Nadia jumped on her bike at seven becomes the first
  double jump. In the dream she moves the way **he remembers her**: the fearless kid on the bike.
- Memories: Walt waving (`mem_walt_alive`), Saturday's newspaper (`mem_newspaper`), "Abernathy Cycles, since 1971"
  (`mem_shop_sign`), and **Walt's repair ledger** (`mem_ledger`): "Sat. E., rear tyre, flat. Patched." written 41 times
  on the same date.
- Deductions: `ded_walt_dead` (the card + Walt alive); `ded_same_saturday` (the paper + the ledger: "He's lived this
  Saturday 41 times. One for every night since the crash.").
- **Battle: Walt**, at his workbench (written out in full in `ARGUMENT-BATTLES.md`). He teaches the verbs.
- **Escape:** the dream notices her. The lawns roll up behind her like carpet and the houses fold flat like a stage
  being struck. A short, safe chase to the end of the street. Mags pulls her up.

**What's learned:** Walt is a memory; the dream is a loop; Elias is there and keeps his distance.
**What changes:** the dream knows a stranger is inside. Tomorrow the neighbours will know her name.
**Spectrum:** the first Force choice is small and sensible (press Walt, or force the garage lock instead of finding
the key). The first Belonging choice is smaller (sit on the porch swing to get your breath back; take the lemonade).

### Night 2: The House
**The day (Day 2).**
- **Corinne Vasse** visits room 14 with fruit he can't eat. LeBaget will move Elias to **Halcyon**, its long-term
  facility, free, "in five days". The board has agreed; LeBaget's partnership grant keeps St. Brigid's open. She asks,
  gently, "Has he said anything?" and says "We lost two of ours that night." **The clock starts.**
- **Rafael** (optional, staff room): he was on when they brought Elias in. Elias said one thing in the trauma bay:
  **"Did Lena get out?"** (`fact_did_lena_get_out`). Rafael also says, generously, that Nadia "was better at the
  practical" in the interview. (She'll learn on Day 3 that the panel split.)
- **Optional, across town (costs two time slots):** Lena's studio, a shared kiln co-op run by **Ines Varga**. Lena's
  shelf of unfired bowls. Ines, carefully: Lena "hadn't been in much this last month", though she told Elias she was
  working late on Thursdays (`fact_lena_thursdays`). Ines gives Nadia Lena's spare house key.
- **Optional, after dark:** bed 3 on the closed wing. A man on the bridge rig, his chart "J. Cho, diver". Someone went in
  and didn't come out (`fact_bed3`). Mags finds her there: "I said don't."
- The duty trade-off begins in earnest (see `NIGHTS-GAMEPLAY.md`): every trip off the ward is a round Rafael covers.

**The dive.** The street again, shorter: the neighbours now wave and say "Morning, Nadia!" (cute, and wrong). Then
**the house**: the Lasname family home rebuilt, noon, rain coming. Lena and Theo inside, making pancakes. The house
won't let her in straight: hallways stretch, a door leads back into the kitchen. **Wallrun** arrives on the stretched
hallway ("Walls in there are only as solid as he remembers them.").
- **Theo** wants to play hide-and-seek (optional, and the best way to find memories). He labels everything; he draws
  monsters. His height marks on the kitchen doorframe stop at 10.
- **The table is set for four.** The fourth chair is for Nadia, who stopped coming to Sunday pancakes after she
  started at St. Brigid's. Sitting in it and eating is comfort (Belonging).
- **The calendar** on the fridge: every Thursday has a little key drawn on it, in Elias's hand. Dream-Lena: "Elias
  drew those. I never asked." (She's his memory; he noticed and never asked either.)
- Theo asks the question for the first time: **"Is Dad going to be sad?"** The answer is remembered (honest, comfort,
  or silence). An honest answer is what later brings Theo to her side.
- Deductions: `ded_she_knows` (Lena knows what she is); `ded_saturday_off` (in the dream Elias is home on Saturday,
  but the real Elias worked that day: "This isn't a memory. It's the Saturday he didn't have.").
- **Battle: Lena**, at the kitchen table. Midway, **she tells Nadia what Nadia never let herself put together: "He
  called you first. You didn't pick up. So he called me."** (If the player already combined the call log and the
  voicemail on Day 1, Nadia can concede it: "I know." Lena respects that; it opens her partner path.) During her
  pressure, Theo's drawings slide across the table. One shows "THE BAD DREAM": a woman in scrubs.
- **Escape:** the house tips. Floors become walls, the staircase becomes a slide, the hallway turns over while she
  runs along it. Out the front door, into the rain.

**What's learned:** the dream's people know what they are; the defences are a grieving mind protecting the only
place his family is alive. **She is the nightmare.** And the chain of the night: no answer, so Lena, so Theo.
**What changes:** the dream defends itself by name. Lena watches from the window from now on. Mags's lines get
quieter. The Long Night's slope gets steeper (the fourth chair is always set).

### Night 3: The Shift
**The day (Day 3).**
- **The hand:** the surgeon's note in room 14 (`fact_hand`): crush injury, tendon repair, poor prognosis for fine
  motor function. Nadia knows what that means for an ER doctor before the player does: "He won't intubate again."
- **His office** (required; Rafael has the key): on the desk, his own **Advance Care Planning form**, dated the night of
  the crash, 2:47 AM. "Health-care proxy: Nadia Lasname." The wishes section blank. One witness signature: R. Duarte.
  Void without a second (`fact_proxy`). Rafael, if asked: "He said, don't tell her, she'll think I'm dying. I thought
  it didn't count."
- **Optional:** the hiring file in the same office (`fact_hiring_file`): panel split two to two, the founder's casting
  vote; and six months later, a new senior post funded from "the founder's discretionary fund", given to Rafael. The
  remark was half true, and he knew it, and he tried to make it even.
- **Optional (with Lena's key):** the empty Lasname house, sealed and dusty. Theo's room. Lena's coat with a parking
  stub from a district across the river, dated a Thursday. A letter to Elias from the Lasname Trust's lawyers,
  unopened, about "your father's remaining interests". Seeds only.
- **Optional:** if Nadia has neglected duties, Rafael confronts her at the nurses' station (a short waking-world
  argument; no pressure phase, because the waking world has none).
- **Evening, the phone ritual.** The first evening the Long Night can lock in, if Belonging is already at its
  threshold (section 7).

**The dive.** The street is shuttered now (the dream has gone defensive); Lena watches from the window. Past the
house, where the dream's map should end, is **St. Brigid's as Elias dreams it**: pastel, quiet, every bed holding a
smiling patient, every monitor drawing a perfect line. In his hospital nobody dies on his shift.
- **The two moments:** Nadia's own ID badge, swiped at a ward door, flips the hospital between **the dream's shift**
  (empty corridors, locked doors) and **the shift as it was** (crowded, loud, doors open, gurneys everywhere). Same
  walls, two moments (Titanfall 2's *Effect and Cause* idea, with a badge instead of a time device).
- In "as it was", a staff-room door plays **the remark**, as Elias remembers it: what he said, and what he saw on her
  face. Watching it is optional; walking away from it is comfort.
- **Her locker** in the dream hospital holds a letter: "Appointed on merit." Taking it is the strongest comfort yet
  (Belonging).
- **Battle: the Doctor**, in the trauma bay, at the lightbox. Elias as he was on shift: sharp, impatient, right about
  medicine. His claim is a clinician's: "You don't bring a patient back to a life he wouldn't want." Its last premise
  is the remark itself, aimed at her right to decide. The honest answer concedes the half truth and then plays the
  proxy form: "You broke the tie. And then you chose me anyway, to decide exactly this."
- **Escape: code blue.** The perfect monitors flatline one after another down the corridor (in his hospital someone
  is finally dying: him). Gurney slides, a lift-shaft chimney climb, the badge flipping the corridor mid-run.

**What's learned:** the dream is the Saturday he should have had (no shift, no remark); he trusted her to decide.
**What changes:** the hospital's floor gives way; the road is underneath. Mags, at the monitor afterwards: "One
more night is safe. Two isn't."

### Night 4: The Road
**The day (Day 4; short and tense).**
- Elias's vitals have dropped. Corinne brings the transfer papers; the date is fixed for day 6.
- **Optional:** Route 9. Six weeks on, Larpaca security still holds a cordon: helmets, drones, no faces. A shrine of
  notes and flowers from St. Brigid's patients against the barrier (`fact_shrine`: "You're the reason I'm alive, Doc").
  The skid marks show the car was stationary when it was hit (`fact_car_stopped`). One LeBaget wreath. No Larpaca one.
- **Mags tells bed 3** (if Nadia has her trust): Julian Cho went in after his wife, eight months ago. She woke. He asked
  to stay, and Mags let him. "That's the last time I decided anything for anyone."
- **Evening, the phone ritual.** If Belonging is at its threshold (and the Long Night didn't lock on Day 3), this is
  where it locks: "Your phone is at 3%. The charger's across the room. Leave it?"

**The dive.** Straight down: the street dark for the first time, the house's windows lit, the hospital's basement, and
then **Route 9 at 3:48 AM**, raining, looping. The war is dream-weather: two colours of searchlight sweeping the road,
tracer fire falling like slow paper streamers, corporate billboards (LeBaget, Larpaca) lining the road.
- **Theo** (if earned on Night 2) waits at the top of the road with a torch: "I know the shortcuts." He runs ahead and
  lights the route: the game's "follow the light", made a child. Without him the route is lit only by the sweep of
  the headlights.
- **The chase:** the car's headlights come down the road behind her, looping, and she runs ahead of them: over cars,
  along billboards, under barriers. Three legs with two breathers.
- **The phone rings in her pocket** in the middle of the chase: "Elias". Answering slows the world, and his voice says
  "You still good for picking me up?" If she says "On my way", the dream rewrites the night for her: she's driving,
  the rain eases, everyone's safe. It's beautiful, and it skips the reveal. (In the Long Night route both options say
  "Answer".) Letting it ring keeps her running.
- **The reveal**, at the crash point, for those who let it ring: the world freezes. The car is stopped. The passenger
  door opens. Lena steps out into the light, one hand raised, and walks toward it. The light takes her. Then the
  shooting. It's Elias's memory, and he was concussed by then: it may be what happened, or what he needed to see.
- **Theo's question, the second time**, at the guardrail: "If he wakes up, he'll know I'm gone. Is Dad going to be
  sad?" A conversation with one rule: only honest answers count (this teaches the **Tell** verb). An honest answer
  gives **Theo's blessing** (`truth_theo`: "Tell him it's okay to be sad.").
- **Force route:** Lena breaks the rules here (section 7).
- At the end of the road, Elias sits on the guardrail beside the car. He looks up: **"Nads?"** Mags pulls her out:
  "Not tonight. His heart."

**What's learned:** the reveal; Theo's blessing; that Elias, deep down, knows she's here.
**What changes:** the final conversation is set. Every gate for the endings is now fixed (section 10).

### Night 5: The Car
**The day (Day 5; short; everything optional).** The transfer is tomorrow. Nadia can say what she wants to say to
Rafael, Ines, Corinne or Mags, chart her brother one last time, and sit in room 14. The voicemail is still "Not now."
In the Long Night route the day is wrong (section 7).

**The dive.** The dream doesn't fight her any more: the street is empty and dim, the house is dark, the hospital
silent. The road is quiet; the rain is soft. The car is parked on the shoulder, engine off, the dashboard clock at
3:48. Elias is in the driver's seat. **Nadia opens the passenger door and gets in.** She's picking him up.

The final talk uses the battle's verbs with one rule change: there's no pressure phase, because he isn't fighting
her (until and unless she presses). His claim is "Let me stay." The verbs she learned from Walt on Night 1 are now
the endings (section 9).

## 5. The layers
1. **Action (everyone gets this):** break into a dream, outrun it, win arguments, bring your brother home. A complete
   story on its own.
2. **Inversion (Night 2):** you're the intruder. The defences are a mind protecting itself, and a ten-year-old drew you
   as the monster.
3. **The question (Night 3):** he named her to decide and left the answer blank. Is it her call? What would he want?
   Whose good is she serving?
4. **Hidden (for players who look):** she heard him ask; the 2:47 form; the tie-break and Rafael's post; Lena's
   Thursdays; the father's money; the two framings of the news; why LeBaget wants his body. None of it is resolved.
5. **Aimed at the player:** the game spends four nights teaching you to **win** arguments and to get very good at
   breaking into a grieving man's mind. Its best ending asks you to stop winning and **tell**. Mags notices, on
   Night 4: "You've gotten very good at this."

## 6. The three hinted threads (layer 4)
**The rules:** each thread gets seeds in both worlds, never a confirmation. No character ever explains one. The one
reveal (Night 4) reframes "Lena missing" without answering it. Seeds are never required for an ending, except where
noted as alternatives.

|---|---|---|---|
| **Opening** | The headline: "WIFE MISSING" | The anchor says crossfire; the headline says "gang violence" | "The Good Doctor" |
| **N1** | Police report: passenger door open, "missing" | LeBaget's flowers in room 14 | The founding plaque: he was 29. In the dream, the father's house on the hill: gate chained |

Corinne recognises Mags in a corridor: "Dr. Ilunga. I didn't know you were still practising." |

**What a careful player might suspect, and the game never confirms:** Lena knew someone at that roadblock; the Lasname

knows, or what he owns, or what his father did. Several readings fit each seed, on purpose.

## 7. The Force and Long Night spectrum
Two hidden values, at opposite ends of one line: **Force** (Nadia imposing her will on the dream) and **Belonging**
(the dream accepting her, the slope to the Long Night). Honest play sits in the middle. Neither is shown as a number.

```
FORCE  <-------------------  honest  ------------------->  BELONGING
press, tether, break in        ask, concede, tell          rest, accept, give, stay
Morning (forced)            Morning (chosen) / Evening           the Long Night
```

**Where each slope starts, and how it feels:** both are rewarded in play, which is what makes them slippery.

| Night | Force (loud; Nadia doubts herself every time) | Belonging (quiet; it feels like kindness) |
|---|---|---|
| **N1** | Press Walt (a faster battle). Force the garage lock instead of finding the key | Rest on the porch swing (restores composure). Take Mrs. Lindqvist's lemonade |
| **N2** | Press Lena. Ask Mags to Tether you out of a bad moment (it costs his heart) | Sit in the fourth chair and eat. Tell Theo "No, sweetie, he won't be sad". Give the dream a memory to skip a hard stretch of the escape |
| **N3** | Press the Doctor. Rip through a stage flat as a shortcut in the escape | Take the "Appointed on merit" letter. Walk away from the remark instead of watching it |
| **Every evening** | | Leave the phone off the charger |
| **Every day** | Lie to Rafael | Skip duties to sit in room 14 |

- **Doubt lines:** at Force 2, 4 and 6 Nadia stops: *"Is this for him?"* The player can carry on or call it off (Force
  goes down by one; the guardian on that layer remembers that she stopped).
- **Fairness:** the memory-for-a-shortcut offer never replaces the assist option. A struggling player can always ask
  Mags to "lower the dream's pressure" with no story cost; giving a memory is a deliberate choice, and it removes that
  entry from the journal (so it can't be used in an argument).
- **The Long Night threshold:** Belonging 7 (out of about 14 available by Day 4). Hard to reach by accident; easy to
  reach for a player who keeps choosing comfort.
- **The lock-in:** every evening has the phone ritual ("Plug it in?"), so it's routine. On the first evening (Day 3 or
  Day 4) when Belonging is at the threshold, the prompt is "Your phone is at 3%. The charger's across the room. Leave
  it?" **Leave it** locks the Long Night. **Plug it in** pulls Belonging back by two, and the prompt never returns.
- **The Force route:** Force 5 or more by Night 4. It doesn't lock an ending; it changes how the dream treats her.

**The two rule-breaks (the one deliberate rule-break, per route):**
- **Force route: Lena.** Lena stops obeying the game. From Night 3 she appears outside her layer: the pursuer in the
  code-blue escape; a page of Nadia's journal in Lena's handwriting ("Leave him alone."); in an argument, her
  patterns come from outside the arena and slide over the verb buttons; on Night 4 she stands in the road. Once, on
  Day 4 at the Route 9 cordon, Nadia glimpses long blonde hair in the crowd and loses it. A dream figure loose in the
  waking world, or the real Lena: the game doesn't say.
- **Long Night route: the art rule.** Style E's rule is that billboards exist only in the dream. After the lock-in, the
  waking world starts breaking it: Rafael is a flat cut-out at the nurses' station; a corridor is a painted flat; the
  board in room 14 reads "Saturday". Waking facts fade from the journal. Only Mags stays solid. On Night 5 the dive
  doesn't fight her at all.

## 10. The endings in full
### Prerequisites at a glance
| Ending | Needs | Locked out by |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning, forced** | Press in the car (any route but the Long Night) | the Long Night lock-in |
| **Morning, chosen** | The confession, plus two of Theo / Lena / Purpose (three in the Force route), then ask him | the lock-in; not enough releases (he chooses to stay) |
| **Evening** | Let it rest; **or** ask him, he chooses to stay, and she accepts | the lock-in |
| **The Long Night** | Belonging at threshold on Day 3 or 4, and "Leave it" at the phone | plugging the phone in |

### Morning, chosen (the old "His Choice", shown)
1. **The car.** She tells him (Tell, one truth at a time; the creator writes these). He tells her his side: he drove, he
   insisted. They find out they've been carrying the same night. She asks, "What do you want?" A long beat on the
   dashboard clock. It moves: 3:49. "...Drive me home, Nads?"
2. **The road.** They get out. If Lena is a partner, she's at the roadside; if not, she's in the window of the house
   later. Theo is at the guardrail. Elias kneels. Theo: "Are you going to be sad?" Elias: "Yes. For a long time." Theo:
   "Okay." (Theo's question, answered by the one person it was for.)
3. **The climb (played).** The two of them run up through the layers **together**: road, hospital, house, street. Not
   a chase: the dream releases them, each layer gently struck behind them like a stage set. Elias runs beside her
   (the movement at its most joyful; he's fast, he whoops on a wall jump). Walt waves from the garage. The neighbours
   line the street. The music in full.
4. **The booth.** Nadia wakes. Mags, at the monitor, very quietly: "...His eyes are open."
5. **Room 14.** He can barely speak. His first words: "Where's Lena?" Nadia: "We don't know." (Unresolved, on screen.)
6. **Months later (four or five shots).** Theo's grave; beside it, a stone bench with Lena's name and no dates. Elias's
   hand in a brace, teaching residents instead of working the floor. A missing poster for Lena on the staff board.
   In the lobby a new plaque: "St. Brigid's, in partnership with LeBaget." (Did he take their money? Unresolved.)
7. **The last scene.** Night. Nadia's phone on her nightstand, charging. It rings: "Elias". **She answers on the first
   ring.** "You still good for picking me up?" "I'm already outside." Headlights pull into St. Brigid's. Hold on her
   phone: the old voicemail, now marked played to the end. (When did she listen? With him, off screen. The creator
   may want to show it.)

- **What the player sees:** a man choose life, at cost, and the ritual of the opening restored.
- **Unresolved:** where Lena is; who fired; whose money keeps St. Brigid's open.
- **Nadia's guilt: resolved, between them.** He knows she heard and didn't come. She knows he drove. In the car he
  says the remark wasn't true; she says "It's half true." He: "Rafael was good." She: "He was." They laugh. Conceding
  the half truth is what finally takes its sting out.
- **The hope variant** (if she used "She got out of the car"): the same ending, but the last shot before the phone call
  is Elias driving Route 9 at night, alone, slowly, looking. It's a life. It might be a cage.

### Morning, forced
1. **The car.** She presses: "You're coming back. I'm not asking." Each press cracks the windscreen further. He
   doesn't fight; he just says "Nads, don't." (If she asked first and he said "I want to stay", this lands harder.)
2. **The collapse (played; the game's hardest run).** The dream comes apart toward the route. Nadia drags Elias up
   through every layer by the hand: he's heavy, he stumbles, she can't double jump while holding him and must
   choose where to let go and catch him again. The guardians react to how she treated them: Walt (convinced) holds
   the street open, "Go on, I'll patch it"; Walt (pressed) lets the garage door slam. In the Force route Lena pursues
   them, then appears **ahead**, breaking every rule.
3. **The tear.** At the end of Larch Street the sky rips. Mags: "Now." White.
4. **Room 14.** He wakes gasping, terrified, into the grief like a fresh wound: the dream gone, the loss new. "Where's
   Theo? ...Where are they?"
5. **Months later.** He lives; he recovers. He's courteous to Nadia and far away. He sleeps with the lights on. She
   still picks him up. On one drive he says, to the window: "I had the strangest dream. You were in it." A pause. "I
   wish you'd let me finish it." (Or the creator's line. It must not be a verdict: he might be grateful, too.)
6. **The last scene.** She drops him off and sits alone in the car. She plays the voicemail at last and hears the
   apology, from a man who is alive and won't talk about that night. The dashboard clock turns 3:48 to 3:49.

- **What the player sees:** a life saved and a choice taken. The game doesn't say it was wrong; the proxy form says he
  trusted her to decide.
- **Unresolved:** the threads, and whether he'll ever forgive her for something he doesn't quite remember.
- **Nadia's guilt: not resolved, and now doubled.** She carries the night of the call and the night she broke his
  dream. She hears his forgiveness for the first, alone, with no one to tell about the second.

### Evening
1. **The car.** She lets it rest. She doesn't argue. She winds the window down. The rain stops. The dashboard clock
   moves past 3:48 for the first time. The light changes: dusk. The car is parked on Larch Street, porch lights
   coming on. **The endless Saturday finally gets its evening.**
   (His way: she asked, he said "I want to stay", and she said "Okay." Same scene, one line different.)
2. **The walk (played, slow).** She walks him up the street. Her movement is gone: the double jump does nothing.
   There's nowhere to run to. Neighbours on their porches. Walt pulls the garage door down: "Evening, Nads." Lena
   and Theo at the door. Elias goes in. Nadia doesn't. Theo waves from the window.
3. **The porch.** She sits on the steps until the lights inside go out, one by one. Mags: "Nadia?" "I'm coming up."
4. **The days after.** His heart slows over nine days. Halcyon's transfer is quietly withdrawn ("We'll leave him with
   his family," Corinne says, and never says why LeBaget no longer wants him).
5. **The last night.** Room 14, the lights down. Nadia takes out her phone and plays the voicemail, all of it, for the
   first time, at his bedside: "...It's not true, and it was rude of me to even imply it..." On the monitor his
   heartbeat slows like someone falling asleep after a good day.
6. **After.** Two graves and an empty stone. Nadia back on shift at St. Brigid's. A letter from the Lasname estate, now
   addressed to her, unopened in her drawer next to the old "return to sender" one.

- **What the player sees:** a man allowed his peace, and a sister who heard his apology too late to answer it.
- **Unresolved:** all three threads, and now the money is hers to refuse again.
- **Nadia's guilt: forgiven, not lifted.** She hears that the remark wasn't true and that he was sorry. She also chose
  his death. The game doesn't say if she was right.

### The Long Night
1. **After the lock-in** the route goes wrong (section 7): the waking world flattens into billboards; Day 5's board
   says "Saturday"; her journal's waking facts fade; only Mags stays solid. Mags notices, and says less and less.
2. **Night 5's dive** doesn't fight her. The neighbours wave. Walt has fixed her old bike. Lena has set the fourth
   plate. In the car, Elias says "Stay with me." Every premise can only be conceded: her journal is blank. She concedes.
3. **The reversed chase (played).** The morning comes for her: white light down Larch Street, Mags's voice calling her
   name, her alarm ringing somewhere. She runs **away** from waking, toward the house. The pursuer never catches
   her, even if she stops: the dream won't let it. That's what makes it wrong.
4. **3 AM, in the dream.** Her phone rings: "Elias". She answers on the first ring. "On my way." She drives to St.
   Brigid's, picks him up, and they drive home along a Route 9 with no lights in the road. Lena and Theo asleep
   upstairs. The clock resets. Saturday morning.
5. **The booth (waking).** Mags alone at three monitors now: bed 3, room 14, and the chair. She plugs in Nadia's dead
   phone. It lights: "1 voicemail. Elias. Played to 0:09." Mags's last line (the creator's). She moves Nadia to bed 4.

- **What the player sees:** a perfect ending inside a horror ending.
- **Unresolved:** everything, and now nobody is left looking for Lena.
- **Nadia's guilt: resolved only in the dream,** where she picked him up. She never hears the apology.

## 11. The creator's question: is "His Choice" shown?
**Yes, both ways.** The proposal shows his decision on screen: if he chooses to wake, Morning (chosen) plays in full,
with the climb, the waking and the epilogue; if he chooses to stay, Evening plays in his version. That's what lets it
merge into Morning without becoming a fake choice: it's his, so it can go against her. What he decides follows from
what she brought him (section 9), and the journal shows her what she has before she gets in the car, so the outcome is
earned, not random.
