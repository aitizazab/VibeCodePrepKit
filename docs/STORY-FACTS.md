# Visiting Hours: the fact catalogue

You asked for this in STORY-PROSE (line 152): every fact and clue, where and when it's found, what it's used for,
and whether it's required, optional or worldbuilding. It's built from the current story (STORY-BIBLE,
NIGHTS-GAMEPLAY, ARGUMENT-BATTLES), as of 2026-10-11.

**Kinds.**
- **Required:** the story or a main path can't do without it.
- **Optional:** it helps an argument or an ending, but there's another way.
- **World:** worldbuilding or a seed for one of the three hinted threads (where Lena is, the crossfire, the money).

**FLUX** marks every row your `//` notes cut, reject or call into question; the reason follows.

**References.** SB = STORY-BIBLE, NG = NIGHTS-GAMEPLAY, AB = ARGUMENT-BATTLES, PROSE = STORY-PROSE, with line numbers.
The options for each FLUX row are in `STORY-TALK.md`.
