### Why the voicemail says "played to 0:09" when it plays to the end while she sleeps
- **Docs say:** her phone shows "Elias, 0:41, played to 0:09" (SB:106), and the Long Night's last image uses the same

- **Extrapolation, three fixes:**
  1. She taps it off as she drops the phone. Then the rest of the message can't play in the room, and the opening loses

  2. Keep the auto-play. The phone shows the message as played, and "0:09" becomes her own memory or journal note
     ("heard up to..."), not a phone label. The "Not now" stays, because she knows she never heard it.
  3. Keep the auto-play and drop the timestamp. The hook is that she has never listened to it awake, not a number.
- Your falling-asleep change (her eyes close, the voice fades to nothing, then the cut to the top-down phone) works
  with fix 2 or fix 3.
