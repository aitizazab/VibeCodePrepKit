## Nadia Lasname (the protagonist)
**Personality.** Dry, guarded, precise. Funny in a way that keeps people at arm's length. Calm in a crisis and
clumsy in a conversation. When she's hurt she goes quiet, and when she's very hurt she goes **unreachable**: phone
off, door shut, "I was asleep". Charts everything, including herself.

**History.** The younger Lasname. Grew up in the big house at the top of Larch Street, and spent her childhood at the
bottom of it, in Walt's bike shop, ringing every bell on the display (hence "Bell"). Fearless on a bike; jumped the
creek at seven. At 20 she refused her allowance and her share of the family trust (why, she's never fully said: "I
wanted a life I paid for"), and when their father died three years ago she sent the estate's letter back unopened.
Public nursing college, then years of agency shifts across the city. Two years ago she applied for a staff post at
St. Brigid's. She got it. The day before the crash, Elias implied it was because she's his sister. He'd broken a
two-two tie on the panel; she'd half suspected it; he said it out loud.

She picks Elias up after his late shifts because he doesn't drive after nights. That night she heard him ask, heard
him give her an out, and took it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | Her brother back |
| **Need** | To say out loud what she did, and to be reachable again. And to accept being *as good as*, not better than: the half truth |
| **Flaws** | Goes dark when hurt (the phone is the symbol). Proud to the point of refusing help. Keeps score. Treats kindness as charity, so she's coldest to the people kindest to her (Rafael). A fluent liar when she's protecting someone, including herself |

**Feats and skills (what she can do).**
- **Nursing:** reads a chart and a monitor at a glance; triage; an IV in the dark; calm in a code. In play: she **charts**
  (the journal), she **reads wristbands and vitals** (duties, and evidence in the Doctor's argument), and she knows
  what "poor prognosis for fine motor function" means before the player does.
- **Noticing:** the charting habit is how facts enter the journal, in her clipped voice.
- **In the dream:** she moves the way Elias remembers her, as the fearless kid on the bike. That's the in-world reason
  for the double jump, the wallrun and the speed: "In there, you're as light as he thinks you are." (Mags)
- **Arguing:** a nurse's honesty and a sibling's aim. She knows exactly where to press, which is the problem.

**Secrets (hints only).** She heard the first lines of the voicemail and chose sleep (only the player knows). She has
never played the rest. She suspected the tie-break before he said it.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competent, closed. Treats the dive like a procedure | Learns he called Lena because she didn't come. Learns she's the nightmare | Learns he trusted her to decide. Faces the remark, and the half truth | Sees Lena walk into the light. Answers a child honestly, or doesn't | Picks him up. Tells him, or presses, or lets him go, or stays |

**Voice.** Short sentences. Clinical words when she's frightened ("Charting:"). Swears softly. Calls him "Elias" when
she's angry and "E" when she isn't. Never says "sorry" first. Her journal is written like nursing notes.
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* Journal: "Charting: patient's sister has not slept.
  Patient's sister is fine."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* To Mags: "Tell me what it costs him. Not the brochure."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* To Walt: "You let me ring the bell before anyone paid."

## Elias Lasname
**Personality.** Brilliant, impatient, blunt. Generous with money and stingy with praise. Says the cruel true thing
because it's true and never the kind true thing because it's kind. Hates being thanked; can't sit still; fixes things
when he can't fix feelings. Protective in a way that makes the protected person feel small.

**History.** Spent ages 8 to 16 in Walt's shop rather than the house on the hill ("Dad worked from home and didn't want
us underfoot"). Medicine; ER. Took his share of the family money and, at 29, used it to turn a shut clinic into **St.
Brigid's**, a community hospital in a city where those don't survive. Founder, attending, fundraiser, the man who
launched Advance Care Planning Week and never filled in his own form until 2:47 AM on the last night. Married Lena
eleven years ago (he stitched her hand in an ER after she cut it on a broken pot). Theo, ten. Doesn't drive after
night shifts since a tired driver died on his table. That night he drove anyway, because Lena offered and he said he
was fine.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want (in the dream)** | The Saturday he didn't have: home, no shift, no remark, his family |
| **Need** | To forgive himself, and to let someone help him (the hardest thing he can do) |
| **Flaws** | Pride disguised as competence. Avoids instead of apologising (he stayed late rather than face her; he apologised to a voicemail). Controls (he drove). Noticed Lena's Thursdays and never asked |

**Feats and skills.** Emergency medicine (fast, blunt, right); built and runs a hospital (money, politics, charm when he
bothers); fixes bikes (Walt's lessons; it's how he calms down). In the dream he's the architect: every defence is his
mind's.

**His two dream forms.**
- **Soft Elias** (the dream's host): relaxed, laughing, always at the barbecue or in the next room, the man he wishes
  he were. Never in the same room as Nadia until Night 4.
- **The Doctor** (Night 3's guardian): Elias as everyone saw him on shift, in his white coat, sharp and right about
  medicine. The dream keeps him inside the shift it deleted, with the remark.

**Secrets (hints only).** The 2:47 proxy form. Rafael's post, funded from his own discretionary fund (paying off the
tie-break). What he knows about his father's money ("Never ask about Dad's business," he told Nadia once). The little
keys he drew on Lena's Thursdays. That he insisted on driving.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seen at a distance, laughing, unreachable | Always in the next room: a coat on the hook, a laugh upstairs | The Doctor argues his case and makes the remark again | On the guardrail: "Nads?" | In the car. Decides, or is decided for |

**Voice.**
- **Awake / the Doctor:** clipped, medical shorthand, sarcasm as a reflex, verdicts not questions. Never "sorry" to a
  face.
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Sentiment isn't a treatment plan. Next."
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "You're here because I broke a tie. Don't make me
    regret it."
- **Soft Elias / the car:** easy, warm, unhurried; says the kind things he never said; goes quiet instead of sharp.
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Nads! You came. Grab a plate, there's too much."
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "She offered to drive. I said I was fine."

## Lena
**Personality (as Elias remembers her).** Quick, warm, teasing, plain-spoken. Laughs at her own jokes before the end.
Stops joking mid-sentence when something matters. Fierce about the people she loves. In the dream she's slightly too
perfect, because she's what he kept, and **she knows it**.

**History (proposal).** Ceramicist: bowls and glaze tests, a shelf at a shared kiln co-op run by Ines Varga. Met Elias
with a cut hand. Eleven years married. Liked Nadia; missed her when the Sunday pancakes stopped. In the last month she
told Elias she was working late at the kiln on Thursdays; Ines says the kiln was cold. On Route 9 she got out of the
stopped car and walked toward the light (if Elias's memory is right). She was never found.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want (dream-Lena)** | To keep his peace, and to keep the only version of her that's left |
| **Need (dream-Lena)** | To let him go, which is really his need to let her go |
| **Flaws** | Protects by keeping out. Makes it personal (she aims at Nadia's absence, and she's right) |

**Feats and skills.** In the dream: the house obeys her (rooms rearrange, doors lead back to the kitchen). She knows
the dream's rules better than anyone but Elias. In the waking world, only traces: glaze recipes, unfired bowls,
a coat, a parking stub.

**Secrets (hints only).** Where she went on Thursdays. Why she got out of the car. Whether she knew anyone at the light.
Ines: "She said your family's money had a smell. She never said of what." Dream-Lena knows none of it: she's built
from what Elias knew, and he never asked.

**Arc across the five nights.**

|---|---|---|---|---|
| A figure in a window | The guardian: "He called you first." Knows what she is | Watches from the window (Force route: starts breaking rules) | The reveal: walks into the light. Partner: holds the road. Force route: blocks it | Partner: in the back seat, adds her truth. Otherwise at the door at the end |

**Voice.** Short, warm, direct; calls Nadia "Nadia" in full (only Elias says "Nads"); calls Elias "love" without
irony.
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Syrup's on the left. Theo hid the good one again."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "I know what I am. I'm what he kept. Don't take that too."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "He called you first, Nadia."

## Theo

question the adults are walking around. Suspects he's a dream and has been told not to say it at the table.

**History.** Born 2016. Learned to ride without stabilisers last summer, taught by Aunt Nadia, Walt's way. Wanted to be
"a doctor for monsters". Asleep in the back seat on Route 9.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | For Dad not to be sad. For Aunt Nadia to be on his team |
| **Need** | An honest answer |
| **Flaws** | A child's: he hides when things get loud (literally: hide-and-seek is how he copes) |

**Feats and skills.** Knows every shortcut in the dream (children's maps of a place are better than adults'). On Night 4
he's the guide: runs ahead with a torch, lights the way, finds hidden memories. His drawings are slips in Lena's
battle; one of them is "THE BAD DREAM", a woman in scrubs.

**Secrets (hints only).** He drew a key on a page of his sketchbook too, the week before. He doesn't know why Mum did it.

## Mags (Marguerite Ilunga)
**Personality.** Frank, dry, kind without being soft. A scientist who talks about dreams like plumbing. One
superstition: she never says "the nine"; she says "the bad ones". The cold coffee is a habit she's stopped noticing.

**History (proposal).** A neurophysiologist who ran a sleep-research programme at a corporate lab (which, she won't
say). The programme ended after the bad ones. Elias, who'd heard of her, gave her the closed sleep-research wing at
St. Brigid's and paid for the rig, in cash, no questions on either side. 212 dives. Nine bad ones. One of them is
**Julian Cho**, in bed 3: he went in after his wife; she woke; he asked to stay, and Mags let him. She visits him every
night.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To keep the bridge alive (it dies with Elias's money) and to get Elias back (she owes him) |
| **Need** | To forgive herself for Julian, and to stop hiding behind "it's not my call" |
| **Flaws** | Refuses to decide for others, which is its own way of leaving them alone. Needs Elias alive for selfish reasons too, and says so once |

**Feats and skills.** Reads an EEG like sheet music; rebuilt the rig from salvage; knows the dream's rules. In play:
- **Briefings** that teach the rig, movement and the journal;
- **Foresight** on Nights 1 to 3: she reads his vitals and calls the dream's next move ("It's folding left!"), an audio
  cue in escapes;
- **Tether** (E): a half-pull that freezes the dream for 3 seconds. It costs his heart and counts as Force;
- **the assist option:** "I can turn the dream's pressure down." No story cost.

**Secrets (hints only).** Where the rig came from. Julian. Corinne knows her: "Dr. Ilunga. I didn't know you were still
practising."

## Walt Abernathy
**Personality.** Patient, slow-spoken, practical. Believes anything can be fixed with patience, and has been proven
wrong once, slowly, every morning for six weeks. Gentle even when he's in the way.

**History.** Born 1941. Opened Abernathy Cycles at the bottom of Larch Street in 1971. Never married; the street's
uncle. Taught both Lasname kids to fix and ride bikes; Elias every afternoon for eight years, Nadia whenever she could
get past the bells. Died in February 2015, while Elias was in medical school (he missed the funeral for an exam; a
small thing, never said, a hint of why Elias brings him back).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To keep the boy safe on his street |
| **Need** | To admit that patching the same tyre isn't fixing it |
| **Flaws** | Patience as avoidance. He'd rather fix the flat forty-one times than look for the thorn |

**Feats and skills.** A mechanic's eye for what's actually wrong. He **teaches the argument** on Night 1, in his own
terms: a wobbling wheel, a loose spoke, truing. His repair ledger is a key piece of evidence.

**Secrets (hints only).** He knows he's a memory. He knows about the hill house and doesn't talk about the father ("Your
dad paid his bills on time. That's all I'll say about your dad.").

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The guardian; teaches the verbs | Waves her through: "Morning, Bell." | Patching the shuttered street | Gone quiet; the garage is dark | Holds the street open (forced, if convinced), waves (chosen), pulls the door down (evening), fixes her bike (Long Night) |

**Voice.** Slow, kind, mechanic's metaphors that he actually means. Calls Nadia "Bell".
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Easy. Watch the pattern first."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "When a wheel wobbles you don't hit it. You find the
  loose spoke."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Forty-one times. Same tyre. You'd think I'd learn."
