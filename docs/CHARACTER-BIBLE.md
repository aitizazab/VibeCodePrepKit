## Nadia Lasname (the protagonist)
**Personality.** Dry, guarded, precise. Funny in a way that keeps people at arm's length. Calm in a crisis and
clumsy in a conversation. When she's hurt she goes quiet, and when she's very hurt she goes **unreachable**: phone
off, door shut, "I was asleep". Charts everything, including herself.

**History.** The younger Lasname. Grew up on Larch Street, and spent her childhood at the bottom of it, in Walt's bike
shop, ringing every bell on the display (hence "Bell", unconfirmed). Fearless on a bike; jumped the creek at seven.
Public nursing college, then years of agency shifts across the city. Two years ago she applied for a staff post at St.
Brigid's. She got it. The day before the crash, Elias implied it was because she's his sister. It's half true: she was
qualified, but the other candidate was just as good. He'd broken a tie on the panel; she'd half suspected it; he said it
out loud. Since the crash she **refuses to use his money while he's in a coma** (the creator).

She usually picks Elias up from work. That night she played his voicemail, heard him ask whether she was still picking
him up, and fell asleep as it played on to its end. Since then she carries **survivor's guilt** (the creator): she
thinks the world would have been better off if Lena had been the one who was spared. What Lena did or was that makes that
make sense is undecided, and so is the siblings' history with Lena.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | Her brother back |
| **Need** | To put down her survivor's guilt (what exactly it rests on is undecided). And to accept being *as good as*, not better than: the half truth |
| **Flaws** | Goes dark when hurt. Proud to the point of refusing help. Keeps score. Treats kindness as charity, so she's coldest to the people kindest to her (Rafael). A fluent liar when she's protecting someone, including herself. Bad habits and an unhealthy schedule (on the Long Night route they leave her open to her guilt) |

**Feats and skills (what she can do).**
- **Nursing:** reads a chart and a monitor at a glance; triage; an IV in the dark; calm in a code. In play: she **charts**
  (the journal), she **reads wristbands and vitals** (duties, and evidence in the Doctor's argument), and she knows
  what "poor prognosis for fine motor function" means before the player does.
- **Noticing:** the charting habit is how facts enter the journal, in her clipped voice.
- **In the dream:** she moves the way Elias remembers her, as the fearless kid on the bike. That's the in-world reason
  for the double jump, the wallrun and the speed: "In there, you're as light as he thinks you are." (Mags)
- **Arguing:** a nurse's honesty and a sibling's aim. She knows exactly where to press, which is the problem.

**Secrets (hints only).** She played the voicemail and fell asleep while it ran on (only the player hears the rest). She
has never heard the apology; when and where she does is undecided. She suspected the tie-break before he said it.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competent, closed. Treats the dive like a procedure | Sits down for pancakes and plays hide-and-seek, waiting for Elias, who never comes. The Night 4 truth is undecided | Convinces him (Morning, chosen), forces him out (Morning, forced), fails to convince him (Evening), or, on the Long Night, loses and takes his hand |

**Voice.** Short sentences. Clinical words when she's frightened ("Charting:"). Swears softly. Calls him "Elias" when
she's angry and "E" when she isn't. Never says "sorry" first. Her journal is written like nursing notes.

## Elias Lasname
**Personality.** Brilliant, impatient, blunt. Generous with money and stingy with praise. Says the cruel true thing
because it's true and never the kind true thing because it's kind. Hates being thanked; can't sit still; fixes things
when he can't fix feelings. Protective in a way that makes the protected person feel small.

**History.** Spent ages 8 to 16 in Walt's shop. Medicine; ER. At 29 he used the family money (whether he inherited it
as the eldest son or earned it is unclear) to turn a shut clinic into **St. Brigid's**, a community hospital in a city
where those don't survive. Founder, attending, fundraiser. Married Lena eleven years ago. Theo, ten. That night he
stayed late at work, putting off facing Nadia, left her the voicemail, and called Lena. The creator: who drove "doesn't
matter".

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want (in the dream)** | The Saturday he didn't have: home, no shift, no remark, his family |
| **Need** | To forgive himself, and to let someone help him (the hardest thing he can do). His guilt, in the creator's words: "Guilty for getting his wife and son killed in his mind." Whether he represses it or comes to it over the nights, and what releases it: undecided |
| **Flaws** | Pride disguised as competence. Avoids instead of apologising (he stayed late rather than face her; he apologised to a voicemail). Controls. Noticed Lena's Thursdays (the small stars on the fridge calendar) and never asked |

**Feats and skills.** Emergency medicine (fast, blunt, right); built and runs a hospital (money, politics, charm when he
bothers); fixes bikes (Walt's lessons; it's how he calms down). In the dream he's the architect: every defence is his
mind's.

**His two dream forms** (whether the Doctor is a separate figure from the soft host is undecided).
- **Soft Elias** (the dream's host): relaxed, laughing, always at the barbecue or in the next room, the man he wishes
  he were. On Night 2 Lena keeps him away from Nadia.
- **The Doctor** (Night 3's guardian): Elias as everyone saw him on shift, in his white coat, sharp and right about
  medicine. The dream keeps him inside the shift it deleted, with the remark.

**His decision.** Granular (the creator): anything that could affect it should, and he mentions the major ones.

**Secrets (hints only).** He noticed Lena's Thursdays (the small stars on the fridge calendar) and never asked.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seen at a distance, laughing, unreachable | Never comes to the table: Lena keeps him away | The Doctor argues his case and makes the remark again | On the guardrail: "Nads?" | The last conversation (whether it's in the car is undecided). Decides, or is decided for; on the Long Night he asks her to stay |

**Voice.**
- **Awake / the Doctor:** clipped, medical shorthand, sarcasm as a reflex, verdicts not questions. Never "sorry" to a
  face.
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Sentiment isn't a treatment plan. Next."
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "You're here because I broke a tie. Don't make me
    regret it."
- **Soft Elias:** easy, warm, unhurried; says the kind things he never said; goes quiet instead of sharp.
  - *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Nads! You came. Grab a plate, there's too much."

## Lena
**Personality (as Elias remembers her).** Quick, warm, teasing, plain-spoken. Laughs at her own jokes before the end.
Stops joking mid-sentence when something matters. Fierce about the people she loves. In the dream she's slightly too
perfect, because she's what he kept, and **she knows it**.

**History.** A **stargazer** (the creator): the attic of the family house is her small observatory. She doesn't need to
work (Elias comes from money). Eleven years married. Something about her Thursdays didn't add up: in the dream, every
Thursday on the fridge calendar has a small star on it: her stargazing nights. After the crash on Route 9 the passenger door was standing open. **She was never
found: she's missing, not dead.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want (dream-Lena)** | To keep his peace, and to keep the only version of her that's left |
| **Need (dream-Lena)** | To let him go, which is really his need to let her go |
| **Flaws** | Protects by keeping out: she keeps Elias away from Nadia, afraid Nadia would convince him to leave the place where he's happy. Acts as if she's real when she isn't |

**Feats and skills.** In the dream: the house obeys her (rooms rearrange, doors lead back to the kitchen). She knows
the dream's rules better than anyone but Elias. In the waking world, only traces: the attic observatory, a coat, a
parking stub.

**Secrets (hints only).** Where she went on Thursdays. Where she went from the car. Dream-Lena knows none of it: she's
built from what Elias knew, and he never asked.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A figure in a window | The guardian: admits she won't let Elias go. Ends the argument unsure (open to giving her blessing later) or condemning Nadia; either way the house throws Nadia out | Watches from the window (Force route: starts breaking rules) | The Night 4 truth is undecided. Partner: holds the road. Force route: blocks it | Partner: in the back seat, supporting Nadia's arguments (the creator's idea). Otherwise at the door at the end |

**Voice.** Short, warm, direct; calls Nadia "Nadia" in full (only Elias says "Nads"); calls Elias "love" without
irony.
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Syrup's on the left. Theo hid the good one again."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "I know what I am. I'm what he kept. Don't take that too."

## Theo
**Theo stays** (the creator: "I think he may need to stay"). The creator liked his hide-and-seek on Night 2 and his
torch-lit escape on Night 4; where his presence affects things still to be addressed (his overlap with Lena's character
space, his death in the Morning ending) is to be marked as it comes up.

**Personality.** Ten. Curious, literal, relentless. Labels everything. Draws monsters with names and rules. Asks the
question the adults are walking around. Suspects he's a dream and has been told not to say it at the table.

**History.** Born 2016. Learned to ride without stabilisers last summer, taught by Aunt Nadia, Walt's way. Wanted to be
"a doctor for monsters". Asleep in the back seat on Route 9. Killed in the crash.

| | |

**Feats and skills.** Knows every shortcut in the dream (children's maps of a place are better than adults'). On Night 2
his hide-and-seek is the stealth tutorial. On Night 4 he's the guide: runs ahead with a torch, lights the way, finds
hidden memories.

## Mags (Marguerite Ilunga)
**Personality.** Frank, dry, kind without being soft. A scientist who talks about dreams like plumbing. One
superstition: she never says "the nine"; she says "the bad ones". The cold coffee is a habit she's stopped noticing.

**History (proposal).** A neurophysiologist who ran a sleep-research programme at a corporate lab (which, she won't
say; unconfirmed). The programme ended after the bad ones. Elias, who'd heard of her, gave her the closed sleep-research
wing at St. Brigid's and paid for the rig, in cash, no questions on either side. 212 dives. Nine bad ones. Julian Cho
is cut, with no replacement.

**What she tells Nadia (the creator's scene).** She won't follow Nadia in: more minds in his dream make it less stable.
She gives Nadia "Five Nights, maybe Six before it's too dangerous to continue". In the dream only the Truth is on
Nadia's side. And stay calm: undue distress could do irreversible damage to Elias.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To keep the bridge alive (it dies with Elias's money) and to get Elias back (she owes him) |
| **Need** | Undecided |
| **Flaws** | Needs Elias alive for selfish reasons too, and says so once |

**Feats and skills.** Reads an EEG like sheet music; rebuilt the rig from salvage; knows the dream's rules. In play:
- **Briefings** that teach the rig, movement and the journal;
- **Foresight** on Nights 1 to 3: she reads his vitals and calls the dream's next move ("It's folding left!"), an audio
  cue in escapes;
- **Tether** (E): a half-pull that freezes the dream for 3 seconds. It costs his heart and counts as Force;
- **the assist option:** "I can turn the dream's pressure down." No story cost.

**Secrets (hints only).** Where the rig came from.

**Arc across the five nights.**

## Walt Abernathy
**Personality.** Patient, slow-spoken, practical. Believes anything can be fixed with patience, and has been proven
wrong once, slowly, every morning for six weeks. Gentle even when he's in the way.

**History.** Born 1941. Opened Abernathy Cycles at the bottom of Larch Street in 1971. Never married; the street's
uncle. Taught both Lasname kids to fix and ride bikes; Elias every afternoon for eight years, Nadia whenever she could
get past the bells. Died in February 2015, while Elias was in medical school (he missed the funeral for an exam; a
small thing, never said, a hint of why Elias brings him back).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Want** | To keep the boy safe on his street |
| **Need** | To admit that patching the same tyre isn't fixing it |
| **Flaws** | Patience as avoidance. He'd rather fix the flat forty-one times than look for the thorn |

**Feats and skills.** A mechanic's eye for what's actually wrong. His repair ledger (the same tyre patched 41 times on
the same date) is a key piece of evidence. His battle as the tutorial is rejected as currently built (the creator); what
teaches the argument, and whether his battle survives in another form, is undecided.

**Secrets (hints only).** He knows he's a memory.

**Arc across the five nights.**
| N1 | N2 | N3 | N4 | N5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The first guardian (his battle's form is undecided) | Waves her through: "Morning, Bell." | Patching the shuttered street | Gone quiet; the garage is dark | His part in each ending is undecided (the endings are the creator's; see `STORY-BIBLE.md`) |

**Voice.** Slow, kind, mechanic's metaphors that he actually means. Calls Nadia "Bell" (unconfirmed).
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "When a wheel wobbles you don't hit it. You find the
  loose spoke."
- *(placeholder voice sample, for the creator to rewrite)* "Forty-one times. Same tyre. You'd think I'd learn."
