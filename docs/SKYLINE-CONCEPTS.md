# Skyline Repo: concept bank

Weapon and object concepts the creator kept. Images live in
`FableAnimationTests/art/concepts/skyline/` (not shipped, not in `Assets/`).

## The Writ: courtroom diorama (chosen direction, 2026-10-13)

`writ-courtroom-base.jpg` (round 3, courtroom take 2). A gothic courtroom in miniature grown into a pistol
silhouette: the judge's bench on top at the rear, a stone colonnade with gaslamps along the body, the arched doorway
is the muzzle, a scroll-wrapped stone column is the grip, a brass gavel floats above.

The creator: "Take 2 of the Writ is promising, it just need to remove the judge sitting inside the gun, and make some
clean sights for the gun that fit in thematically." Round 4 does exactly that (prompts in
`FableAnimationTests/.work/writ-prompts/v4/`).

## Clockwork heart (kept for another gun)

`clockwork-heart.jpg` (round 3, take 2). A slowly beating bronze-and-bone heart held by a ribbed handle; a petal
muzzle opens with each beat. Implied sound: a heartbeat thump and ticking at rest, a valve-burst shot, a hiss of
steam. The creator: "quite a cool idea, and even if it's not used for Writ, it might be worth saving as concept art
for another gun."

## The Writ: final design (2026-10-14)

`writ-final.jpg` (round 5, take 1). The creator chose round 4's A take 1 (judge removed, a tall slim brass obelisk
with an amber bead on the archway's keystone as the front sight) and asked for "a circular window behind the chair with
a horizontal line that lines up with the other vertical line to make a cross for the sightlines"; then "just use Take 1
of the round window". The rear sight is a round window through the empty judge's chair with one horizontal brass bar;
through it, the bar crosses the obelisk to make the sight's cross.
